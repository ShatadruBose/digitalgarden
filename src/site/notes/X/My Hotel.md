# Mega-Size Hotel Room

The hotel room is designed as a **mega-sized suite** with a long, sequential layout that separates the entrance/lounge area, circulation corridor, bathroom facilities, bedroom, wardrobe area, washroom, and balcony.

## 1. Front Sitting Area

Immediately after entering the room, there is a **3 m × 3 m front sitting area**, giving approximately **9 m²** of floor space.

This functions as a private lounge or reception area within the suite. It can accommodate:

- Sofa
- Chairs
- Coffee table
- Side tables
- Decorative furniture
- Additional seating or storage

The sitting area provides a clear transition between the hotel corridor outside and the more private areas of the room.

## 2. Main Corridor

From the front sitting area, a **corridor continues forward along the right-hand side** of the room.

This corridor acts as the principal circulation route through the suite. The arrangement allows the bathroom and washroom to be accessed without requiring guests to walk through the bedroom.

## 3. Main Bathroom

On the **left-hand side of the corridor** is the main bathroom.

The bathroom measures approximately:

**2 m × 3 m = 6 m²**

Although the bathroom itself is positioned on the left side of the overall layout, its **entrance door is on the right side**, opening toward the corridor.

The bathroom can contain:

- Wash basin
- Toilet
- Shower area
- Mirror
- Bathroom storage
- Other standard sanitary facilities

Its position near the front of the suite makes it convenient for guests and visitors.

## 4. Main Bedroom

Continuing forward through the corridor, the room opens into the main bedroom.

The bedroom measures approximately:

**3 m × 5 m = 15 m²**

This is the principal sleeping area of the suite.

It can accommodate:

- Large bed
- Bedside tables
- Bedside lighting
- Seating
- Additional storage
- Decorative furniture

The bedroom forms the central private portion of the hotel room.

## 5. Wardrobe / Dressing Area

On the **right-hand side of the bedroom** is a dedicated wardrobe area.

The wardrobe/dressing space measures approximately:

**2.5 m × 2.5 m = 6.25 m²**

This is effectively a separate dressing and storage zone rather than merely a wardrobe placed against a wall.

It can contain:

- Full-height wardrobes
- Drawers
- Shelves
- Luggage storage
- Full-length mirrors
- Dressing table
- Clothing storage

The location beside the bedroom keeps clothing and luggage organized without occupying the main sleeping area.

## 6. Secondary Washroom

Behind the wardrobe area is a separate, substantially larger washroom.

The washroom measures approximately:

**2.5 m × 7 m = 17.5 m²**

This is considerably larger than the main 2 m × 3 m bathroom and functions as a substantial secondary sanitary/washing area.

The washroom extends behind the wardrobe section, creating a long rear service zone.

Importantly, the **washroom entrance also opens toward the corridor**.

Therefore:

- The main bathroom opens toward the corridor.
- The secondary washroom also opens toward the corridor.
- Neither facility requires someone to walk through the bedroom to reach it.

This provides better separation between the circulation areas and the private sleeping area.

## 7. Balcony

At the far end of the bedroom is a large balcony.

The balcony is approximately **5.5 m in length**, based on the stated dimensions of approximately:

**3 m + 2.5 m = 5.5 m**

Its width has not been specified, so its total floor area cannot be calculated from the available dimensions.

The balcony can potentially accommodate:

- Outdoor chairs
- Small table
- Lounge furniture
- Plants
- Decorative elements
- Outdoor seating

It therefore functions as an outdoor extension of the suite rather than merely a narrow balcony.

**Entrance**

**3 m × 3 m Sitting/Lounge Area**
↓
**Right-Side Corridor**
├── **Left: 2 m × 3 m Main Bathroom**

**3 m × 5 m Main Bedroom**
