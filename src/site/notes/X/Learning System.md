# Learning System

## I. Core Learning Process

### 1. Focus
Give the subject your undivided attention and eliminate unnecessary distractions. Deep concentration improves both comprehension and retention.

### 2. Reflect
After learning, pause and examine what you understood, what confused you, and why. Reflection converts information into deeper understanding.

### 3. Apply
Use what you learned through problems, projects, experiments, or real situations. Application reveals whether you actually understand the concept.

---

# II. Learning Framework

## A. Retention & Understanding

### 4. Active Recall
Retrieve information from memory instead of repeatedly rereading it. The effort of recalling strengthens your ability to remember and use knowledge.

### 5. Spaced Repetition
Review information at increasing intervals over time. This fights forgetting while minimizing unnecessary repetition.

### 6. Teach to Learn
Explain a concept to someone else—or teach it aloud to yourself. Teaching forces you to organize your knowledge and exposes gaps in understanding.

---

## B. Improvement & Feedback

### 7. Embrace Failure
Treat mistakes as diagnostic information rather than as evidence of inability. Analyze the failure, correct the cause, and try again.

### 8. Growth Mindset
Assume that abilities can improve through deliberate practice, feedback, and better strategies. Focus on progress rather than a fixed definition of talent.

### 9. Rapid Feedback Loops
Get feedback as quickly as possible after attempting something. Shorter feedback loops allow mistakes to be corrected before they become habits.

### 10. Accelerate Cycle Time
Reduce the time between learning, practicing, testing, receiving feedback, and improving. More useful learning cycles produce faster skill development.

---

## C. Strategic Learning

### 11. The Bottleneck Skill
Identify the single skill or weakness that is currently limiting your progress the most. Improving the bottleneck often produces greater gains than improving already-strong areas.

### 12. Learn from Experts
Study people who have already achieved high levels of competence. Analyze their methods, mental models, mistakes, and decision-making rather than merely copying their results.

### 13. Simplify Everything
Remove unnecessary complexity and reduce concepts to their essential principles. Simple systems are easier to understand, remember, execute, and improve.

### 14. Question Every Requirement
Do not automatically accept that something must be done a particular way. Ask what the requirement accomplishes and whether it can be eliminated, changed, or simplified.

### 15. Extreme Sense of Urgency
Act quickly once the next useful action is clear. Avoid unnecessary delays, while maintaining enough care to preserve quality and accuracy.

---

# III. Critical Thinking System

### 16. Syllogism Sanity Test
Break an argument into its premises and conclusion, then check whether the reasoning actually follows. A logically valid structure still requires true premises.

### 17. Prove Yourself Wrong
Actively search for evidence, counterexamples, and alternative explanations that could invalidate your conclusion. This reduces confirmation bias and strengthens your reasoning.

### 18. Second-Order Effects
Look beyond the immediate result and ask what happens next. Consider the consequences of consequences, including unintended effects.

---

# IV. Memorization Protocol

## The 3–2–1 Method

### 19. Three Strategic Reads
Read the material three times with different purposes: understand the structure, identify the important details, and consolidate the whole concept.

### 20. Two Rounds of Teaching
Teach the material twice without simply reading from your notes. Each round should reveal gaps and force you to reconstruct the knowledge from memory.

### 21. One Honest Write
Close your resources and write everything you can genuinely remember. Compare it with the source afterward and identify exactly what you missed.

---

# V. Complete Learning Loop

**Focus → Learn → Recall → Reflect → Apply → Get Feedback → Identify Bottleneck → Improve → Teach → Review**

> **Goal:** Understand deeply, remember reliably, apply effectively, detect weaknesses, and continuously improve.
