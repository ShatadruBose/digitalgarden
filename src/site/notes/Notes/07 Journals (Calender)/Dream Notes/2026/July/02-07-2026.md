Dream Journal – July 2, 2026

Last night, I had an interesting and unusual dream about my brother. In the dream, he was living with me for most of the months, and only spent a few days staying with other people. It felt completely normal, as if that had always been our routine.

During the dream, we had a playful interaction where I touched his teeth, and he touched my teeth two or three times. It didn't feel strange or uncomfortable in the dream—it was simply part of how we were interacting.

At one point, I was sitting at my computer and playing a game. While I was focused on the computer, I reached over and touched my brother's teeth again. Everything in the dream felt calm and natural, and we continued spending time together without anything dramatic happening.

When I woke up, I remembered these details clearly because they were unusual and stood out from the rest of the dream. Although it was a simple dream, the interactions and the feeling that my brother lived with me most of the time made it memorable.
