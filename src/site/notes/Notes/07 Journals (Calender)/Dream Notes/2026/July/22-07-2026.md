# Dream Note — Today in Minecraft

Today I dreamed that I was in Minecraft, planting flowers in a tiny square patch of land. It was a very small space, only about 3 blocks wide in each direction, and it had been neatly shoveled flat. The little square sat in the middle of a place where roads spread out in every direction, making it feel like a tiny garden crossroads.

Even though the area was small, it felt peaceful and special. I carefully placed flowers one by one, trying to make the space look bright and beautiful. The roads around it made the whole scene feel open, but the flower bed itself stayed compact, simple, and organized.

It was a calm dream, and I liked the feeling of turning a small, plain spot into something colorful and alive.
