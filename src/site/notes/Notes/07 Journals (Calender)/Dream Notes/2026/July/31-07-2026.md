# 🌙 Dream Journal

**Date:** 31 July 2026  
**Type:** School Dream

## Dream

Last night, I dreamed that I was at school after the Physics and Chemistry period. Everyone was packing their bags quickly before the next class. I accidentally picked up someone else's **Physics & Chemistry** book because it looked exactly like mine.

I walked around the school with the book for quite a while without realizing the mistake. Later, during another class, I opened the book and noticed that the name written on the first page was not mine. I immediately became worried and started wondering whose book it was.

I searched around the classroom and nearby corridors until I finally found the student who owned it. They looked relieved because they had been searching for the book. I apologized for the mix-up and returned the book. They smiled, accepted the apology, and thanked me.

After giving the book back, I felt a great sense of relief. The dream ended with both of us returning to class as if nothing had happened.

## Feelings

- 😅 Confused after discovering the mistake.
- 😟 Worried about the missing book.
- 🔍 Determined to find the rightful owner.
- 😊 Relieved and happy after returning it.

## Possible Meaning

The dream may symbolize honesty, responsibility, and the importance of correcting mistakes. Even when an error happens accidentally, making an effort to fix it brings peace of mind.
