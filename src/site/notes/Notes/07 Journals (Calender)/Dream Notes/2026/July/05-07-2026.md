Dream Journal – Computer Backup & Meeting Devangan Balo

Date: 5 July 2026

Last night I had an interesting dream with two distinct parts.

In the first part of the dream, I was sitting at my computer, carefully backing up important files. The process felt very organized and serious. I remember checking folders, selecting files, and making sure everything was copied safely. It seemed like I was protecting valuable data, almost as if losing those files would have been a major problem. The computer worked normally, and I felt focused on completing the backup successfully.

Later, the dream shifted to a different place. I found myself inside a shop. While looking around, I unexpectedly met Devangan Balo. It was a pleasant surprise. We recognized each other and had a friendly interaction. The atmosphere in the shop was calm, and meeting him there felt completely natural, even though it was unexpected. After spending a little time together, the dream gradually faded.

Overall, the dream combined two unrelated experiences: carefully protecting important digital files through a backup process and unexpectedly meeting Devangan Balo in a shop. The dream felt realistic, peaceful, and memorable.
