# Dream: Chess Class

**Date:** 29 June 2026

## Dream

Last night, I dreamed that I was in a classroom where everyone was learning chess. The classroom was quiet and organized, with each student sitting at a desk with a chessboard in front of them. A teacher stood at the front of the room, explaining how the chess pieces move and teaching different strategies.

Everyone listened carefully as the teacher demonstrated openings, tactics, and checkmate patterns on a large demonstration board. The students practiced the moves on their own boards while asking questions whenever they were confused. The atmosphere was calm, focused, and enjoyable, just like a real chess academy.

I watched the lesson with interest and felt like I was learning alongside everyone else. Before the class ended, the dream slowly faded away.
