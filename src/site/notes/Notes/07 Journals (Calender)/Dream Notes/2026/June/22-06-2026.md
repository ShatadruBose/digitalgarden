Dream Note – 22 June 2026

Date of Dream: 22 June 2026

The place seemed almost like a sanctuary or exhibition where many types of snakes lived together.

I spent my time observing the snakes closely and learning about their differences. The journey felt like an adventure and a discovery mission. Although snakes are often associated with fear, in this dream I felt fascinated and interested in them. The experience was memorable because of the great variety of snakes I encountered and the unique place I visited to see them.

Main Theme: Exploration, curiosity, wildlife, and discovery.
