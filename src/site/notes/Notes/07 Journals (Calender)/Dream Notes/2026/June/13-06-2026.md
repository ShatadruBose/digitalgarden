Dream Note – 13 June 2026

Last night, I had a strange and interesting dream.

In the dream, I completely formatted my PC. Everything was wiped clean, and when it started again, it felt like a brand-new computer. The desktop was fresh, there were no files, and everything looked fast and organized. I felt excited, as if I was setting up a new machine from scratch.

After that, I needed to go to school. Instead of using normal roads, I traveled through a huge underground tunnel system. The tunnels stretched far beneath the city and were connected in many directions.

As I walked, I crossed several underground railway tracks. Trains occasionally rushed through the dark tunnels, making loud noises and creating strong gusts of wind. I carefully crossed the tracks and continued my journey.

Further ahead, I encountered underground drains carrying flowing water. Some drains were narrow while others were large enough to walk beside. The atmosphere felt mysterious, like I was exploring a hidden world beneath the city.

The underground route seemed endless, passing through railways, tunnels, and drainage passages before finally leading toward my school. Although the journey felt unusual and slightly dangerous, I remained curious and continued exploring until I reached my destination.

The dream had a unique combination of technology, exploration, and adventure. The freshly formatted PC symbolized a new beginning, while the underground path to school felt like discovering a secret transportation network hidden beneath everyday life.
