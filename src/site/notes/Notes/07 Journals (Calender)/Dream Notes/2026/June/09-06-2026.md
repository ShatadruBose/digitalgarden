## Dream Note – School to Home Ride — 9th June 2026

Today I had a strange and exciting dream. In the dream, I, Shatadru Bose, and Abhiraj Chakraborty were returning home from school. We were riding on two different bikes and speeding down the road. It felt as if our bikes were accelerating to nearly 200 km/h. The wind rushed past us, and everything around us seemed to blur because of the incredible speed.

We continued racing along the roads for quite some time. The journey felt thrilling and unreal, as dreams often do. Eventually, we reached Barrackpore Station. After arriving there, I got off my bike and went inside the station. I felt the need to urinate, so I looked for a suitable place and relieved myself.

The dream ended shortly afterward, leaving behind the memory of a fast-paced ride, the feeling of freedom on the road, and the unexpected stop at Barrackpore Station.
