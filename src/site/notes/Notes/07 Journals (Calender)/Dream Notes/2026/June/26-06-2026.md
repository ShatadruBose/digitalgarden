Dream Note – 26 June

Last night I had a vivid dream in which I was kidnapped. I do not remember every detail, but I clearly remember the feeling of being taken away against my will. The situation felt tense and uncertain, and I kept wondering what would happen next. Throughout the dream, I was trying to understand where I was and how I could escape. The emotions of fear, confusion, and urgency were very strong, making the dream feel surprisingly realistic.

When I woke up, I realized it was only a dream, but its intensity stayed with me for a while. Although unsettling, it was an interesting experience worth recording.
