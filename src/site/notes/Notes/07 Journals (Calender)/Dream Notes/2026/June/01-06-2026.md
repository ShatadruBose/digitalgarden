Dream Entry – 1 July 2026

1 July 2026

Last night I had a strange and mysterious dream that felt like a riddle.

In the dream, I became aware of a taste that seemed to be beyond the ordinary tastes known to humans. I thought of it as the X Taste, or perhaps even the Z Taste. It felt as though it belonged to a category that had not yet been discovered or understood.

At the same time, I found myself near a huge city. The city was enormous, filled with towering buildings and countless people. Suddenly, something catastrophic happened. The city was destroyed. I do not remember exactly how, but the destruction was immense and left the landscape transformed.

Amid the aftermath stood a single person. They seemed calm despite everything that had happened. Looking toward me, they asked a peculiar question:

"If you have tasted all 26 tastes, then what will happen?"

The question echoed through the dream.

I began wondering what these 26 tastes could be. In reality, people usually speak of a small number of basic tastes, but in the dream there seemed to be many more. It felt as if each taste represented a different experience, emotion, truth, or aspect of existence.

The mysterious person did not provide an answer. They only repeated the question, as though it was something I was supposed to figure out myself.

The destroyed city, the unknown taste, and the unanswered question created a feeling of mystery and discovery. Even after waking up, I kept thinking about what it might mean to experience all 26 tastes and what could happen afterward.

The dream ended without revealing the answer, leaving behind only curiosity and wonder.
