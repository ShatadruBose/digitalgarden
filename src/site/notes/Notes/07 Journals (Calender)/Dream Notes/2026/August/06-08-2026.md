# Dream Notes
**Date:** 2026-08-06

## Dream 1: Crossing the Ganga and the Three-Ship Collision
I dreamed that I was crossing the Ganga River to reach Howrah. The river was calm at first, but suddenly three large ships collided with each other. The crash was enormous, creating loud noises and huge waves. Everyone around me was surprised and watched the ships become tangled together. Despite the chaos, I safely continued my journey toward Howrah and observed the unusual event from a distance.

## Dream 2: Finding Gold and Silver with a Metal Detector
Earlier in the night, I dreamed that I was searching for buried treasure using a metal detector. As I walked across an open field, the detector began beeping. I dug into the ground and discovered shiny silver coins, followed by pieces of gold hidden beneath the soil. Each signal led to another exciting find, and I felt like a real treasure hunter uncovering long-lost treasures.
