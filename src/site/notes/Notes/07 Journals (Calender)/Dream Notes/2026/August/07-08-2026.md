# 🌙 Dream Note — 7 August 2026

**Date:** 7 August 2026  
**Type:** Dream  
**Theme:** Technology / Computer / Storage  
**Main Object:** M: Drive — SSD

## 💤 Dream

Last night, I had a strange dream about my computer and my **M: drive**.

In the dream, my **M: drive was an SSD**, and somehow the physical SSD had started to **bend**. It was no longer perfectly flat like a normal SSD. The drive looked as if it had been physically warped or curved.

I remember being particularly concerned because the SSD was the storage device assigned to my **M: drive**. Seeing a storage device physically bending felt extremely unusual and alarming.

I kept looking at the SSD and wondering how it could possibly bend without immediately breaking. The situation felt unrealistic, but inside the dream it seemed completely real.

### 💾 The M: Drive

The most memorable part of the dream was the association between the physical SSD and the **M:** drive.

Normally, a drive letter is simply a logical representation of a storage volume in Windows. But in the dream, the logical **M:** drive seemed to have a physical form — the SSD itself.

The SSD was therefore not just a drive shown in File Explorer. It was an actual physical object that represented my M: drive.

### 🌀 The Bending

The SSD was visibly **bending/warping**.

I noticed that something was wrong with its shape. Instead of being completely flat, it appeared curved, almost as though the circuit board or casing had been flexed.

I was worried about what might happen to the data stored on it.

I remember thinking about whether the SSD would still work if it remained bent, and whether the files on the M: drive would become inaccessible.

The strange part was that the SSD did not necessarily stop working immediately. It was simply physically distorted.

### 🧠 My Reaction

I felt confused and concerned.

My thoughts were focused on:

- Why is the SSD bending?
- Will the M: drive still work?
- Could the data become corrupted?
- Is the SSD physically damaged?
- Should I stop using it?
- How could an SSD even bend like that?

The dream combined something very technical and familiar — computer storage — with something physically impossible or highly unusual.

### 🖥️ Technology Theme

The dream was strongly connected to my interest in computers and storage.

The **M: drive** represented a familiar part of my computer environment, while the bending SSD represented something going wrong with the physical hardware behind it.

It was almost like the dream was showing me the difference between:

> **Logical storage:** `M:`

and

> **Physical storage:** the SSD itself.

But instead of keeping those two concepts separate, the dream visually connected them.

### 🔍 Interesting Detail

What made the dream particularly memorable was that the problem was not simply:

> "The M: drive is corrupted."

Instead, the **actual SSD was bending**.

That made the dream feel much more physical than a normal computer-related problem.

### 🌌 Interpretation

The dream may simply have been my brain combining familiar computer concepts with an unusual physical scenario.

The SSD could represent something that normally provides stability and stores important information, while the bending could symbolize that something normally rigid or dependable has become physically unstable.

However, this interpretation is speculative. Dreams do not necessarily have a fixed meaning.

## 📌 Dream Summary

On **7 August 2026**, I dreamed that my computer's **M: drive was an SSD that was physically bending**.

The SSD was warped instead of flat, and I was concerned about whether the drive would continue working and whether the data stored on the M: drive would remain safe.

The dream was unusual because it turned a normally **logical computer concept — a drive letter — into a physical object**, creating a bizarre situation where the physical SSD representing the M: drive was literally bending.

**Most memorable image:**  
> A physical SSD belonging to my **M: drive**, visibly bent/warped while I worried about the safety of the stored data.
