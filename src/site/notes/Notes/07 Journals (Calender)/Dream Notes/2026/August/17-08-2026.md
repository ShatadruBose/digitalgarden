# Dream Note — Synesthesia

**Date:** August 7, 2026  
**Type:** Dream  
**Subject:** Synesthesia

## Dream

Last night, I dreamt that I experienced **synesthesia**—a strange blending of the senses in which one type of sensory information seemed to trigger another.

In the dream, sounds were no longer simply sounds. When I heard music, I could **see colours** appearing around me. Different notes and voices produced different colours, almost as if the air itself had become a living spectrum. Some sounds appeared as bright flashes, while softer sounds formed gentle, flowing patterns.

Words and numbers also seemed to have their own colours and personalities. When I looked at certain letters, I immediately associated them with particular colours. Numbers felt different from one another as well, almost as though each had its own visual identity.

The experience felt completely natural inside the dream. I did not question why I could see sound or why certain things seemed to have colours. It was simply the way reality worked.

At one point, the dream became especially vivid. I heard a sequence of sounds and watched a complex pattern of colours form in front of me. It felt like my senses were connected together into one larger system rather than being separate.

I remember being fascinated by the experience. It felt as though I had discovered another layer of perception that I normally could not access while awake.

## Feelings

I felt **curious, amazed, and fascinated** rather than frightened. The dream made ordinary things—sounds, words, numbers, and colours—feel much more mysterious and interconnected.

## Interpretation

The dream may have reflected my interest in **synesthesia, perception, neuroscience, and the relationship between different senses**. It was particularly interesting because the dream itself seemed to simulate the idea of experiencing one sense through another.

The strongest image I remember is the idea of **hearing colours and seeing sounds**, as if the boundaries between the senses had disappeared.

## Reflection

The dream made me wonder what perception would be like if the human brain naturally connected sensory information in completely different ways. It also reminded me that our experience of reality is constructed by the brain, and that there may be many possible ways of experiencing the same world.

**Dream significance:** A vivid exploration of sensory perception and the blending of the senses.
