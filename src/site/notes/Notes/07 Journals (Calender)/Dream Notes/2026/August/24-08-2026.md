# Dream Note — 24 August 2026

**Date:** 24 August 2026

I had a strange dream about my computer.

In the dream, I opened my computer after it had supposedly been **factory reset**. I expected everything to be completely wiped—my files, programs, settings, accounts, and all the things that had been on it before.

But when I turned it on, something was immediately wrong.

The computer looked completely normal.

Instead of showing the fresh, clean setup that should appear after a factory reset, it seemed as though **nothing had been erased at all**. My old files and programs were still there, and the computer looked almost exactly as I remembered it.

I was confused because I clearly remembered that the computer had been factory reset. I kept thinking, *How is everything still here? It was supposed to be completely reset.*

I explored the computer, checking different folders and settings to see whether it was somehow a different installation or whether the reset had failed. The more I looked, the stranger it became.

The dream had the unsettling feeling of discovering that something I believed had been completely erased was actually still present.

The main image I remember is **opening a supposedly factory-reset computer and discovering that it wasn't reset at all**.

It felt like the computer had somehow preserved its entire previous state despite being wiped.
