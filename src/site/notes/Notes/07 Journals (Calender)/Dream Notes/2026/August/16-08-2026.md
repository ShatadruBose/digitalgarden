# Dream Note — 16 August 2026

## Overview

On **16 August 2026**, I had a vivid dream centered around **Cities: Skylines II**. In the dream, I was creating and managing three different cities.

## 1. Sexual City

The first city was **[[Notes/05 Thoughts (Resources)/Cities/Sexual City\|Sexual City]]**, a strange and unusual city with a sexual theme.

The dream was physically vivid enough to cause a **nocturnal emission (wet dream)** while I was asleep.

## 2. Mountain City

The second city was **[[Notes/05 Thoughts (Resources)/Cities/Mountain City\|Mountain City]]**, built in a mountainous environment.

During this part of the dream, I also **watched a movie**, with the movie and the city becoming part of the same dream experience.

After the movie, I also saw many dogs in the city.

## 3. Dream City

The third and most detailed city was **[[Notes/05 Thoughts (Resources)/Cities/Dream City\|Dream City]]**, which I designed myself.

Its urban structure was divided broadly by density:

- **Centre:** High-density development.
- **Middle:** Medium-density development.
- **Other areas:** Many pockets of mixed-density development.
- **One corner:** Industrial development.
- **Another corner:** Office development.

### Population Collapse and Recovery

Dream City initially had a population of approximately **131,000**.

Then the city experienced an extreme collapse.

Its population fell from:

**131,000 → 6 people**

At that point, the city had almost completely failed.

However, the city eventually recovered and began growing rapidly again.

The population rose from:

**6 → 200,000 people**

The final population was approximately **200,000**, making the city's journey an extreme cycle of collapse, recovery, and expansion.

## Dream Structure

The three cities represented three distinct parts of the dream:

1. **Sexual City** — the unusual and physically vivid part of the dream.
2. **Mountain City** — a mountainous city associated with watching a movie.
3. **Dream City** — my personally designed city, featuring a planned density structure and an extraordinary population collapse and recovery.

### Most Memorable Event

The most memorable part was **Dream City's population collapsing from approximately 131,000 to only 6 people, and then recovering all the way to approximately 200,000**.

It felt like watching an entire city nearly disappear and then rebuild itself into a major metropolis.
