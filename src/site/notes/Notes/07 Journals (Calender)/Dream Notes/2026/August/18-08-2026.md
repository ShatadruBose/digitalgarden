# Dream Note — 18 August 2026

On 18 August 2026, I had a dream that I was going to tuition. It felt like an ordinary day, and I was getting ready to leave home for my tuition class. I started travelling along the familiar route, but the surroundings somehow felt slightly different from reality.

As I continued towards the tuition, I noticed various people and places along the way. Everything seemed normal, yet there was a strange dream-like feeling to the entire journey. I kept moving forward, thinking about reaching tuition on time.

The journey itself became the main part of the dream. I remember the feeling of travelling towards my destination more than any particular event that happened there. Eventually, the dream faded away.
