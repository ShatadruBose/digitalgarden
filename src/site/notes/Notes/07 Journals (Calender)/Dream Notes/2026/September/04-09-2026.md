# Dream Note — 4 September 2026

**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Location in Dream:** Purulia, West Bengal, India  
**Dream Theme:** Travel, Hotels, Mountains, Nature, Exploration  
**Dream Type:** Travel / Exploration Dream  
**Atmosphere:** Peaceful, green, spacious, scenic, adventurous

---

## 🌄 Overview

On **4 September 2026**, I had a dream in which I went to **Purulia**. The central part of the dream was about travelling there and **choosing hotels to stay in**.

The dream had a strong feeling of being on a journey. It was not simply about reaching Purulia; a major part of the experience was deciding where I would stay. I was exploring different possibilities and looking carefully at the hotels and their surroundings.

Eventually, I chose **two hotels that were located directly side by side**. The two hotels felt almost like neighbouring properties, standing next to each other as if they belonged to the same area or complex.

What made the location particularly memorable was the scenery behind the hotels. The **entire backside was extremely green**, with a large natural landscape stretching beyond the buildings. Behind the greenery were **mountains**, giving the hotels a dramatic natural backdrop.

The combination of hotels, greenery, and mountains made the place feel like a perfect travel destination.

---

# 🧳 Beginning of the Dream

The dream began with me being in **Purulia**.

The overall environment immediately felt different from an ordinary urban setting. There was a strong sense that I had travelled away from the familiar surroundings and entered a place where nature was much more prominent.

The purpose of being there was connected with finding accommodation.

I was essentially looking for a suitable hotel and considering different options. Rather than immediately choosing the first hotel I encountered, I was examining the available places and thinking about where I would stay.

There was a sense of decision-making throughout this part of the dream.

I was not merely a tourist looking at scenery. I was actively involved in **selecting accommodation** for the trip.

---

# 🏨 Choosing the Hotels

The most important event in the dream was the selection of the hotels.

I eventually selected **two hotels**.

The unusual feature was that the hotels were **side by side**.

They were not separated by a large distance. They were immediately next to one another, making it possible to move between them very easily.

The two buildings formed a kind of pair.

From the front, they appeared to be conventional hotels or lodging establishments, but their position became much more interesting when I considered what was behind them.

The fact that I chose two neighbouring hotels gave the dream a strange but interesting sense of organization.

It was almost as though I had deliberately created a small base consisting of two adjacent hotels.

---

# 🌿 The Green Backside

The most visually striking part of the dream was the area **behind the hotels**.

The backside was **fully green**.

Instead of seeing another collection of buildings, roads, shops, or urban infrastructure, I saw an enormous natural landscape.

The greenery seemed to dominate the entire background.

There were trees, vegetation, and open green areas extending behind the hotels. The natural environment felt substantially larger than the built environment.

The hotels therefore appeared almost as if they had been constructed at the edge of a huge natural landscape.

The contrast was particularly strong:

> **Hotels in the foreground → greenery behind them → mountains beyond the greenery.**

This created a layered landscape.

---

# ⛰️ The Mountains

Beyond the green landscape were **mountains**.

The mountains formed the distant background of the scene.

They gave the location a sense of depth. Instead of the view ending immediately behind the hotels, the landscape continued outward toward the hills and mountains.

The mountains made the place feel remote and scenic.

They also changed the character of the hotels.

Without the mountains, the hotels might simply have been ordinary buildings.

With the mountains behind them, they became part of a much larger landscape.

It felt like a place where the hotel itself was only one small component of the environment.

---
