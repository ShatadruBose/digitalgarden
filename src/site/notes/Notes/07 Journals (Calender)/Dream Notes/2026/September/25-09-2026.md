# 🌧️ Dream — The Wooden House in the Mountains

> **Date:** 25 September 2026  
> **Atmosphere:** Dreamcore · Liminal · Surreal · Quiet  
> **Weather:** Rain with thunder  
> **Location:** A wooden house surrounded by mountains and open scenery

## 💤 Dream

I was in a **house made entirely of wood**, situated somewhere with an incredible view of the surrounding landscape.

The house felt unusual. It didn't feel like an ordinary house, and the place seemed almost disconnected from the normal world. The scenery around it was beautiful, with **mountains stretching into the distance** and an enormous open view.

The **front of the house was completely open to the outside**. There wasn't the usual feeling of a closed wall separating the inside from the landscape. The outside seemed to flow directly into the house, making the boundary between the house and the environment feel almost nonexistent.

The **right side of the house was my bedroom**.

It felt like a private little space inside this enormous open environment. From there, I could experience the scenery and the weather outside while still being inside the wooden house.

It was **raining**, and there was **thunder in the distance**. The combination of the rain, the wooden structure, the mountains, and the open front made the entire place feel extremely atmospheric.

The weather wasn't necessarily frightening. Instead, it made everything feel more mysterious and unreal.

The whole environment had a very strong **dreamcore and liminal-space feeling**.

It felt like one of those places that should exist somewhere in the world, but somehow couldn't quite exist normally. Everything looked familiar enough to understand, yet strange enough to feel like I was somewhere between reality and a dream.

The mountains, rain, thunder, wooden house, open front, and isolated bedroom all combined into one strange but beautiful environment.

## 🌄 Environment

- **House:** Wooden, simple, open and isolated
- **Bedroom:** Located on the right side of the house
- **Front:** Completely open toward the outside
- **View:** Vast mountainous scenery
- **Weather:** Heavy rain with thunder
- **Surroundings:** Natural, spacious and seemingly isolated
- **Lighting:** Dreamlike and atmospheric
- **Overall feeling:** Liminal, surreal and strangely peaceful

## 🌩️ Atmosphere

The strongest part of the dream was its atmosphere.

There was something deeply unusual about being inside a wooden house while the entire front of it was open toward the mountains and rain.

The thunder made the environment feel larger and more distant, while the rain gave everything a soft, continuous background sound.

It felt like the house existed in a place **outside normal time and space**.

There was no obvious sense of a city, roads, crowds, or ordinary civilization.

Just:

**a wooden house → an open landscape → mountains → rain → thunder → silence.**

That simplicity made the place feel even more surreal.

## 🌀 Dreamcore / Liminal Feeling

The location felt like a **liminal space** because it seemed familiar but impossible to properly place.

It wasn't clearly a normal home, hotel, cabin, or mountain lodge.

It felt like a place that existed only temporarily for the dream.

The open front of the house especially created the feeling that the inside and outside worlds were blending together.

The mountains looked real, but the entire environment felt slightly detached from reality.

It had the feeling of:

> *"I have never been here, but somehow I remember this place."*

## 🌌 Overall Impression

This dream felt less like a conventional story and more like **experiencing a place**.

The location itself was the main part of the dream.

The wooden house felt like a shelter, while the completely open front connected it directly to the enormous mountainous landscape.

The rain and thunder transformed the scenery into something even more dreamlike.

It felt like I had temporarily entered a **quiet, isolated world that existed between reality and imagination**.
