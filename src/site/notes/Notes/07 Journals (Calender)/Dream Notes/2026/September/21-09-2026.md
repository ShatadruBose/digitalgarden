# 🌙 Dream — 21 September 2026

> [!abstract] Overview
> On the night of **21 September 2026**, I experienced a series of three distinct dreams. Each dream had a different setting and internal logic, but all three involved **hidden or constructed spaces, movement toward a destination, and the act of creating or recording information**.
>
> The first dream involved a **secret room hidden behind the stairs**, connected to a speaker.  
> The second involved **building an Astrology Express** from my house to a destination where I was going to have my astrology checked, together with **Real Civil Engineer**. The construction involved automatically building roads.  
> The third dream was much simpler: **I was writing something on a board**, although the exact content is unclear.

---

# 1. 🏠 The Secret Room Behind the Stairs

## Setting

In the first dream, I discovered or had access to a **secret room located behind the stairs**.

The room was not simply another ordinary room in the house. It felt like a **hidden section of the building**, concealed behind or underneath the staircase.

The stairs acted almost like a barrier or entrance to another part of the house.

There was a strong sense that the room was **secret**.

It was somewhere that could exist within the house without being immediately obvious to someone walking through it.

### The room

The room was positioned:

- **Behind the stairs**
- Hidden from normal view
- Connected to the structure of the house
- Somehow associated with a **speaker**
- Separate enough to feel like a secret area
- Close enough to the ordinary house that its existence felt surprising

The most interesting aspect was that the room was **connected to the speaker**.

The speaker seemed to establish a connection between the hidden room and the normal environment.

It was almost as if the speaker was not merely an electronic device but a **communication link** between the secret room and the outside.

## 🔊 The Speaker Connection

The speaker was an important part of the dream.

The secret room being connected to a speaker created the impression that something could be:

- transmitted from the room,
- heard outside the room,
- controlled through the speaker,
- or communicated between the hidden space and the rest of the house.

The exact technical mechanism did not make complete sense, but the dream accepted it as completely normal.

The room and speaker seemed to belong to the same system.

### Conceptual structure

```text
                HOUSE
                  │
                  │
               STAIRS
                  │
          ┌───────┴───────┐
          │               │
      Normal Space    SECRET ROOM
                          │
                          │
                       SPEAKER
                          │
                          ▼
                 Outside / House
```

## 🕵️ Feeling of secrecy

The strongest characteristic of this dream was **secrecy**.

The room felt like something that was:

> hidden in plain sight.

It was physically close to an ordinary part of the house, yet somehow remained undiscovered or inaccessible.

The staircase therefore became more than just a staircase.

It functioned as a **boundary between the visible house and an invisible/hidden layer of the house**.

### Possible dream themes

- Hidden spaces
- Private systems
- Secret infrastructure
- Discovering something inside an ordinary environment
- Communication
- Concealed technology
- A second layer beneath everyday surroundings

The dream gave the impression that my house contained **more space and infrastructure than was normally visible**.

# 2. 🚆 The Astrology Express

## Setting

The second dream was much more elaborate.

I was together with **Real Civil Engineer**, and we were building something called an:

# **Astrology Express**

The Astrology Express was a transportation system designed to take me **from my house to the place I was going to for checking my astrology**.

Instead of simply travelling there normally, we were actually **constructing the entire route ourselves**.

The dream therefore combined:

- Astrology
- Transportation
- Engineering
- Construction
- Roads
- Automation
- My house
- A destination
- Real Civil Engineer

It was essentially an **engineering project created specifically for an astrological journey**.

---

## 🏠 Starting Point: My House

The Astrology Express began at **my house**.

My house functioned as the origin point of the transportation network.

The route was not already present.

We were building it.

This made the journey different from an ordinary train or road journey.

Instead of:

> House → existing road → destination

the dream operated more like:

> House → construct transportation system → construct roads → travel → astrology destination

---

## 👷 Working With Real Civil Engineer

I was building the Astrology Express **with Real Civil Engineer**.

The presence of Real Civil Engineer was particularly fitting because the project involved:

- infrastructure,
- roads,
- construction,
- engineering,
- transportation,
- and automated development.

The dream therefore treated the construction of the Astrology Express almost like a major civil-engineering project.

It was not simply something we imagined.

We were **actively building it**.

# 🌙 Final Dream Impression

The dreams of **21 September 2026** were strongly centered around the idea of **systems connecting hidden spaces, destinations, and information**.

The first dream placed a hidden technological space inside an ordinary environment: a secret room behind the stairs connected to a speaker.

The second expanded this idea dramatically into infrastructure: an Astrology Express was being constructed from my house toward an astrology-related destination, with roads automatically generating as part of the construction process and Real Civil Engineer participating in the project.

The third reduced everything back to a simple human action: writing on a board.

Taken together, the three dreams moved through three scales:

> **A hidden room → an entire transportation network → a single board.**

The most persistent elements were **space, construction, connection, communication, and information**.
