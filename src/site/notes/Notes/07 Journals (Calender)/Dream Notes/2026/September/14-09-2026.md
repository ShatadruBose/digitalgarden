## 🕐 Dream Overview

I had a disturbing academic dream in which I appeared for my **Bengali Final Examination** and ultimately failed very badly. The examination carried a total of **80 marks**, but when the result was revealed, I had scored only **20 marks**.

The number **20/80** felt extremely low in the dream. It was not a situation where I narrowly missed passing. Instead, the result represented a major failure, making the entire experience feel much more serious and frightening.

The dream was centred around the examination, my performance, the expectation of receiving a much better score, and the sudden realization that my preparation or performance had not been sufficient.

The dream took place in an examination environment.

I was sitting for my **Bengali Final Examination**, with the expectation that I would complete the paper normally and obtain a reasonable score.

The examination appeared to be a formal school examination rather than a casual class test. The word **"Final"** made the examination feel especially important.

There was a strong sense that the marks would matter significantly.

The paper was out of:

**80 marks**

I knew that I was supposed to answer questions carefully and demonstrate my knowledge of Bengali.

The atmosphere around the examination felt serious. There was pressure to perform well, and I was conscious that other students were also writing their papers.
