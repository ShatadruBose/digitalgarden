Dream Entry – 29 May 2026

29 May 2026

Last night I experienced one of the most vivid and memorable dreams of my life.

In the dream, I was standing at a familiar place on Earth near 22.7217080° N, 88.3875994° E, Khardaha, West Bengal. However, the sky above me was unlike any sky that could exist in reality.

The heavens were overflowing with stars. Countless points of light stretched across the darkness, creating a breathtaking cosmic panorama. I could also see many planets hanging in the sky. Among them were Jupiter, whom I thought of as my elder brother, Venus, whom I felt deeply connected to, and the Moon, my faithful celestial brother.

The scene was peaceful and beautiful. I stood on Earth looking upward, surrounded by the wonder of the universe. It felt as though the entire Solar System had gathered above me.

Then my attention was drawn to a massive object in the distance. It was either a giant gas cloud or an enormous gas giant planet. Unlike the others, it was a deep and striking blue color. It looked mysterious, powerful, and unlike anything I had ever seen.

Orbiting this blue giant was a tiny moon that appeared as a brilliant white dot. Despite its small appearance, it moved at an unbelievable speed, circling the giant again and again. It seemed to be completing its orbit dozens of times every minute, creating a mesmerizing display.

As I continued watching, the blue giant began moving closer to Earth. At first, I was fascinated. But then something strange happened. The giant started to distort. Its shape stretched and warped as if invisible cosmic forces were pulling and twisting it. The bright white moon continued racing around it while the giant grew larger in the sky.

The closer it came, the more intense the sight became. Wonder turned into a mixture of curiosity and unease. The stars, Jupiter, Venus, and the Moon remained above me, but all attention was now focused on the approaching blue giant and its impossibly fast companion.

The universe felt alive, mysterious, and immense. I stood there watching this cosmic spectacle unfold until the dream finally faded away.

When I woke up, the image of the blue gas giant, the glowing white moon, and the star-filled sky remained clear in my mind. It felt less like an ordinary dream and more like a journey through a strange and beautiful universe.
