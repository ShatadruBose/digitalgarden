# Dream Note — 5 October 2026

**Date:** 5 October 2026  
**Type:** Dream  
**Theme:** Football / Competition / Progression System

## Dream

I had a dream about a strange football system called **WSL**, with channels ranging from **WSL1 all the way to WSL14**.

However, these were not normal football channels. They were more like **levels in a massive football competition**. Each WSL channel represented a different stage of progression, and reaching the next one required actually defeating the challenge of the previous stage.

At the beginning, **WSL1 was freely accessible**. Anyone could see it, enter it, and compete there without having to unlock anything first. It was essentially the starting level of the entire system.

But once someone wanted to progress to **WSL2**, they had to defeat the **WSL1 challenge**. Until they had successfully defeated WSL1, they could not even see or access the WSL2 channel.

The same rule continued throughout the entire system:

**WSL1 → defeat WSL1 → unlock WSL2 → defeat WSL2 → unlock WSL3 → ... → defeat WSL13 → unlock WSL14.**

The higher the WSL level, the harder the competition became.

## WSL Upgrades

Every time someone successfully progressed from one WSL channel to the next, they received significant upgrades.

The upgrades included:

- **More strength**
- **More armor**
- **More damage**
- **More resistance**
- **More absorption**
- **More regeneration**
- **More health**

So progressing through the WSL system did not merely mean gaining access to a new football tournament. It actually made the competitor **stronger and more capable of surviving the increasingly difficult challenges**.

The system therefore felt almost like a mixture of **football, a tournament ladder, and a video game progression system**.

## WSL14

The final level, **WSL14**, was extremely difficult.

There were **40 WSL14 bosses**.

What made the structure even more complicated was that **each WSL14 boss had four WSL13 children**.

Therefore, every boss had its own group of four WSL13-level opponents connected to it. The entire WSL14 stage felt enormous and almost impossible to complete.

The structure was something like:

```text
WSL14
├── Boss 1
│   ├── WSL13 Child 1
│   ├── WSL13 Child 2
│   ├── WSL13 Child 3
│   └── WSL13 Child 4
├── Boss 2
│   ├── WSL13 Child 1
│   ├── WSL13 Child 2
│   ├── WSL13 Child 3
│   └── WSL13 Child 4
├── ...
└── Boss 40
    ├── WSL13 Child 1
    ├── WSL13 Child 2
    ├── WSL13 Child 3
    └── WSL13 Child 4
```

That meant the final stage was not simply one final match. It was a huge network of difficult opponents and bosses.

## Meaning of WSL

In the dream, WSL did not mean a normal football league or a specific existing competition.

The word WSL represented all football tournaments in Europe, while excluding international events.

For example, the concept covered European club football competitions, but international events such as UEFA's national-team competitions were outside the WSL system.

The dream therefore created its own fictional football universe where European football was organized into a gigantic progression-based competition.

## Overall Feeling

The most interesting part of the dream was the idea that knowledge of a higher level itself was locked behind achievement.

You could not simply look at WSL2 because you had not earned access to it yet. You had to prove that you were capable of handling WSL1 first.

Every level therefore represented both a football challenge and a power upgrade.

The progression felt like:

> Compete → defeat the challenge → unlock the next WSL → become stronger → face harder opponents → repeat.



By the time someone reached WSL14, they would have accumulated enormous upgrades in health, strength, armor, damage, resistance, absorption, and regeneration.

And with 40 WSL14 bosses, each connected to four WSL13 children, the final stage was an almost absurdly difficult ultimate challenge.

It was a strange combination of European football, competitive tournaments, RPG progression, boss battles, and locked-access channels. The dream made football feel less like simply watching or playing matches and more like entering a gigantic world where every victory physically upgraded the competitor and unlocked another part of the football universe.
