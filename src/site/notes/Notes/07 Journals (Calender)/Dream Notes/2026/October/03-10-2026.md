# Dream Note — 3 October 2026

## Dream

I had a dream on 3 October 2026 where my mother sold her phone. After she sold it, a huge argument broke out. The situation became very tense, and there was a lot of disagreement about her decision to sell the phone.

The argument continued for some time, with everyone reacting strongly to what had happened. The situation felt chaotic and emotionally intense.

Eventually, my mother returned the phone. After she got the phone back, the argument and tension began to settle down. The situation felt more normal again.

## Key Events

- My mother sold her phone.
- A huge argument happened after she sold it.
- The situation became very tense and emotional.
- My mother eventually returned the phone and got it back.
- The conflict settled after the phone was returned.

## Feelings

The dream felt intense because the simple act of selling a phone suddenly turned into a major argument. The return of the phone felt like the resolution of the conflict.

## Reflection

The dream may represent how an ordinary decision can sometimes create unexpected conflict, followed by an attempt to restore things to how they were before.
