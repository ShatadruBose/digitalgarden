# MathLang

### Version 1.0 (Concept Paper)

**Author:** Shatadru Bose (Concept)
**Language Name:** MathLang
**Type:** Formal Mathematical Language
**Purpose:** Universal logical, mathematical and scientific communication.

---

# Abstract

MathLang is a symbolic language built upon mathematics instead of natural words.
Unlike English, Spanish or Bengali, every statement in MathLang is based on
logic, sets, functions and symbols.

The objective of MathLang is to become a language where humans and machines
communicate with perfect precision and zero ambiguity.
