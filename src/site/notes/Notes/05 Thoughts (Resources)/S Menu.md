# Ultra Note on the S Menu

The **S Menu** is a grand, multi-layered food structure designed to create a complete dining experience from the first bite to the final sweet finish. It begins with strong starters, moves into rich pre-table combinations, continues into the full main course, and ends with a massive dessert section that gives the meal a luxurious and memorable ending. This menu is built for variety, balance, taste, texture, aroma, and satisfaction. It covers vegetarian, egg, fish, chicken, and mutton choices, so every guest can find something that fits their appetite and preference.

---

## 1. Starters

Starters are the opening act of the meal. Their job is to awaken the appetite, excite the taste buds, and prepare the eater for the heavy dishes that follow. A good starter should be crisp, flavorful, fresh, and visually appealing. The S Menu gives a very wide selection of starters across different food groups.

### A. Veg Starters
These are light, crunchy, and comforting vegetarian starters that work well as snacks or as the first stage of a larger meal.

- **Cheese Ball** – Golden on the outside, soft and cheesy on the inside. It is rich, creamy, and highly popular among people who enjoy molten textures.
- **Staff Potato** – A potato-based starter with a stuffed or shaped format, usually soft inside and crisp outside.
- **Tornado Potato** – A dramatic spiral-cut potato snack, deep-fried or baked, often coated with seasoning for extra flavor and crunch.
- **Potato Pakora** – Slices of potato dipped in seasoned gram flour batter and fried until crisp, giving a warm, earthy, and satisfying snack.

### B. Egg Starters
Egg starters are flavorful, protein-rich, and often preferred for their simple but powerful taste.

- **Egg Pakora** – Boiled egg coated in spiced batter and fried, producing a crunchy shell and tender center.
- **Fish Ball** – Soft fish-based balls, usually seasoned and fried or cooked in a light sauce; they are savory and delicate.
- **Fish Kabab** – Minced or shaped fish preparation, grilled or fried with spices, herbs, and a smoky edge.
- **Fish Finger** – Long, breaded fish strips with a crisp crust and tender interior, ideal for dipping and sharing.

### C. Chicken Starters
Chicken starters are among the most loved items in any menu because of their deep spice absorption, juicy texture, and wide flavor possibilities.

- **Chicken Pakora** – Chicken pieces coated in a spiced batter and deep-fried for a crunchy and juicy result.
- **Chicken Seekh Kabab** – Minced chicken molded on skewers, seasoned heavily, and grilled for a smoky, charred flavor.
- **Chicken Reshmi Kabab** – Soft, creamy, and very tender kabab prepared with yogurt, cream, and delicate spices.
- **Hariyali Chicken Kebab** – Green-herbed chicken kebab made with coriander, mint, and aromatic seasoning, giving a fresh and vibrant taste.
- **Chicken Tikka** – Classic marinated chicken chunks roasted or grilled until they are charred at the edges and juicy inside.
- **Kalmi Chicken Kebab** – A royal-style chicken kebab, usually leg-piece based, richly marinated and cooked for a deep layered flavor.
- **Chicken Galawat Kebab** – Exceptionally soft and melt-in-the-mouth chicken kebab, spiced and shaped into delicate patties.
- **Chicken Shami Kebab** – Soft minced chicken patty mixed with lentil and spice, pan-fried to a golden finish.

### D. Mutton Starters
Mutton starters are heavier, deeper in taste, and often more luxurious than other starters. They carry strong aroma and intense seasoning.

- **Mutton Seekh Kabab** – Skewered minced mutton grilled to bring out smoky richness and robust flavor.
- **Galouti Kebab** – Extremely soft, fine-textured kebab famous for its melt-like mouthfeel and refined seasoning.
- **Shami Kebab** – A round, flattened kebab prepared from minced mutton and lentil, crisp outside and soft within.
- **Kakori Kebab** – A delicate, refined mutton kebab known for its tenderness and elegant spice profile.
- **Mutton Boti Kabab** – Cubes of mutton marinated and cooked until flavorful, smoky, and substantial.
- **Chapli Kebab** – Spiced, flattened minced mutton patty with a bold rustic character and strong seasoning.
- **Barrah Kebab** – A premium mutton kebab often made with rib or chop-style cuts, rich, juicy, and festive.

## 2. Pre Table

The pre-table section is meant to build appetite and offer combination plates before the main course arrives. These dishes often combine bread, curry, fried items, and light accompaniments. They are ideal for guests who want a satisfying prelude instead of waiting for the full main course.

### A. Butter Naan Combinations
Butter naan is soft, buttery, and slightly chewy, making it an ideal partner for rich Indian curries.

- **Butter Naan with Paneer Butter Masala** – Soft naan paired with a creamy tomato-cashew paneer curry.
- **Butter Naan with Butter Chicken** – One of the most famous combinations, pairing rich naan with mildly spiced chicken in a buttery gravy.
- **Butter Naan with Aloo Dum** – A comforting vegetarian pairing with soft potato curry.
- **Butter Naan with Malai Kari** – A luxurious pairing involving rich, creamy curry.
- **Butter Naan with Dal Makhani** – A deeply satisfying comfort combination with slow-cooked lentils, butter, and cream.

### B. Snack Plate Combinations
These are filling and convenient combinations that work well as pre-course food.

- **Kachori with Cholar Dal** – Crisp kachori served with Bengal-style chana dal; warm, spicy, and comforting.
- **Chole Bhature** – A bold North Indian classic with puffed fried bread and spicy chickpea curry.
- **Fish Fry + Egg Devil** – A strong mixed plate of fried fish and stuffed egg devil, combining crunch, spice, and protein.
- **Chowmin + Gobi Manchurian** – A popular Indo-Chinese combination with noodles and saucy cauliflower fritters.

The pre-table section is important because it gives the dining experience momentum. It is not just food before food. It is the bridge between the appetite and the full meal.

---

## 3. Table / Main Course

The main course is the heart of the S Menu. This is where the meal becomes full, complete, and deeply satisfying. The main course should have balance: rice or pulao as the base, curry as the center, and side elements like papad and chutney to complete the dish.

### A. Steam Rice Combinations
Steam rice is the most neutral and flexible base. It absorbs the flavor of curries beautifully.

- **Steam Rice with Chicken Curry** – Simple, strong, and universally loved.
- **Steam Rice with Mutton Curry** – Richer and heavier, suitable for those who want a more intense meal.
- **Steam Rice with Veg Curry** – Light, balanced, and suitable for a vegetarian main course.

### B. Pulao Combinations
Pulao has its own fragrance and flavor, so it can be paired with both dry and gravy-based dishes.

- **Pulao with Aloo Dum** – A flavorful vegetarian combination where spiced potato curry meets aromatic rice.
- **Pulao with Paneer Butter Masala** – Creamy, rich, and indulgent.
- **Pulao with Dal Tadka** – A comforting and wholesome pairing.
- **Pulao with Mutton Kasha** – A bold, hearty, and festive combination for serious non-vegetarian dining.

### C. Fried Rice Combinations
Fried rice brings a slightly smoky and savory character. It works especially well with spiced dry or semi-dry dishes.

- **Fried Rice with Shahi Paneer** – A rich vegetarian pairing with creamy paneer curry.
- **Fried Rice with Chili Chicken** – A powerful Indo-Chinese style combination, spicy and energetic.

### D. Biryani Combinations  

Biryani is one of the most powerful and complete main-course dishes. It combines aromatic rice, rich spices, and deeply cooked meat into a single, layered preparation. It stands between pulao and curry, delivering both fragrance and intensity in one plate.  

- **Mutton Biryani with Chicken Chaap** – A grand and indulgent pairing where rich, slow-cooked mutton biryani is served with flavorful, creamy chicken chaap, creating a deep, festive combination.  
- **Chicken Biryani** – A classic favorite made with spiced rice and tender chicken, offering a balanced, aromatic, and satisfying meal on its own.

### E. Side Additions
Side items complete the main course and improve the overall dining rhythm.

- **Papad + Aam Chatni** – Crispy papad with tangy mango chutney; a perfect sharp contrast to rich gravies and rice dishes.

The main course is the structural center of the S Menu. It should feel complete, balanced, and worthy of a full meal experience.

## 5. Final Meaning of the S Menu

The S Menu is not just a list of dishes. It is a full culinary architecture. It begins with excitement, rises through rich combinations, reaches its peak in the main course, and closes with a dessert universe. It is designed to be flexible, festive, and complete. Veg, egg, fish, chicken, and mutton are all included. Light snacks and heavy dishes both exist. Sweetness is not afterthought; it is a major chapter. That makes the S Menu a powerful menu concept for a large meal, a special event, a restaurant style service, or a personal food framework.

In simple terms, the S Menu is built to satisfy every stage of hunger:
- first, it attracts attention,
- then it builds appetite,
- then it delivers fullness,
- and finally it finishes with sweetness and memory.

That is what makes it complete.
