# **Lad: A Proposed English Equivalent of "Faltu"**
*A Concept Paper on a User-Created Meaning*

**Author:** Shatadru Bose (Concept Originator)

---

# Abstract

This paper presents a proposed semantic extension of the English word **"lad"** to represent the colloquial South Asian concept of **"faltu"**. In Hindi, Bengali, and several other Indian languages, *faltu* commonly means **useless, unnecessary, pointless, worthless, or someone who wastes time without purpose**. While English has many words with similar meanings, there is no single informal word that captures every nuance of *faltu*. This proposal suggests using **lad** in this specialized sense as a modern slang expression.

> **Note:** This is a newly invented meaning and is **not** part of standard English usage.

---

# Definition

**Lad (noun, adjective, slang – proposed)**

**Meaning:**

A person, object, activity, or idea that is unnecessary, pointless, useless, or simply "faltu."

---

# Etymology

This meaning is an original semantic invention. It intentionally assigns a new slang meaning to the existing English word **lad**, independent of its traditional meaning ("a boy" or "young man").

## As a Noun

Refers to a useless or pointless person.

Example:

(Proposed meaning: Don't be so pointless or useless.)

## As an Adjective

Describes something unnecessary.

Example:

- "That's a lad idea."

(Proposed meaning: That's a pointless idea.)

# Limitations

The word already exists in English with the established meaning **"boy" or "young man."** Because of this, using **lad** to mean *faltu* may confuse readers unless the context clearly indicates the intended slang meaning. Adoption would require consistent community use before it could gain wider recognition.

# Conclusion

This proposal introduces a new slang meaning for the English word **lad**, using it as an equivalent of the South Asian term **faltu**. The intent is to provide a concise, informal expression for describing people, actions, ideas, or objects that are unnecessary, pointless, or lacking value. As with all neologisms, acceptance depends on repeated use within a speech community. Until then, this remains an original lexical proposal rather than a recognized definition in standard English.
