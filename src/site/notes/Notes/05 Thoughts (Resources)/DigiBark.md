# DigiBark
## A Complete Constructed Digital Canine Language

**Version:** 1.0  
**Language Family:** Artificial Digital Language  
**Script:** Latin (Dog Sounds)  
**Direction:** Left → Right

---

# Overview

DigiBark is a fully translatable language built entirely from dog-inspired sounds.

Every word is composed only of:

- bark
- woof
- arf
- ruff
- yip
- yap
- awoo
- grr
- huff
- sniff

No other roots exist.

---

# Language Rules Summary

- Subject–Verb–Object order.
- No articles.
- Past tense = `ruff-`.
- Future tense = `awoo-`.
- Negation = `grr-`.
- Plural = `-yap`.
- Possession = `-sniff`.
- Questions end with `yip?`.
- Every word is derived only from the ten root sounds:
  - bark
  - woof
  - arf
  - ruff
  - yip
  - yap
  - awoo
  - grr
  - huff
  - sniff

This makes DigiBark fully systematic, expandable, and completely translatable while remaining entirely composed of dog-inspired digital vocalizations.
