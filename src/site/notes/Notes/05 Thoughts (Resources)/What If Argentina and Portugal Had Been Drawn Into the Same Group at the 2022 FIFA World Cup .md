# What If Argentina and Portugal Had Been Drawn Into the Same Group at the 2022 FIFA World Cup?

## An Alternate History Paper

---

# Abstract

The 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar is remembered as one of the greatest tournaments in football history. Lionel Messi fulfilled his lifelong dream by winning the World Cup, Cristiano Ronaldo played in what would become his final World Cup, Morocco became the first African nation to reach the semifinals, and the tournament ended with arguably the greatest World Cup Final ever played.

This paper explores an alternate timeline in which Argentina and Portugal were drawn into the same group during the group-stage draw. Portugal emerged as group winners, Argentina qualified as runners-up, Portugal's tournament ended in the quarterfinals against Morocco, and Argentina went on to defeat France in the legendary 3–3 World Cup Final on penalties.

---

# Introduction

Before the World Cup draw, many football fans hoped to see Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo face each other at least once in football's biggest competition.

In reality, this never happened because Argentina and Portugal were placed in different groups and different halves of the knockout bracket.

In this alternate history, fate creates one final World Cup meeting between the two greatest footballers of their generation.

# Lessons from This Alternate History

1. The Group of Death lives up to expectations.
2. Portugal proves to be the strongest group-stage team.
3. Argentina demonstrates resilience after a difficult start.
4. Morocco produces another historic upset.
5. Knockout football rewards mentality as much as talent.
6. Messi and Ronaldo finally meet in a FIFA World Cup.
7. Argentina's championship becomes even more remarkable after surviving such a difficult group.

# Conclusion

This alternate version of the 2022 FIFA World Cup would have added another unforgettable chapter to football history. Portugal's triumph in the group stage would showcase their quality, yet Morocco would still halt their campaign in the quarterfinals with another historic upset. Argentina, forced to battle through adversity after finishing second in the toughest group, would grow stronger with every match.

The tournament would still culminate in the unforgettable World Cup Final between Argentina and France—a breathtaking 3–3 spectacle decided by penalties. Lionel Messi would lift the FIFA World Cup trophy, completing one of the greatest careers in the history of football, while Cristiano Ronaldo's final World Cup would end in heartbreak despite Portugal's impressive group-stage performance.

In this alternate history, the journey changes—but the ending remains legendary.
