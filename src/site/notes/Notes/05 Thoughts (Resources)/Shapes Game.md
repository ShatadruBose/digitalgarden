# Shapes Game
## A Strategic Manufacturing, Economics, and Geopolitics Simulation

**Version:** 1.0 (Concept)
**Genre:** Educational Strategy, Economics, Engineering, Team Competition
**Duration:** 2 Hours
**Players:** 40 (4 Teams × 10 Players)
**Currency:** United States Dollar (USD)

---

# Abstract

Shapes Game is a competitive educational simulation combining geometry, manufacturing, economics, teamwork, logistics, and strategic decision-making. Players represent citizens of different nations belonging to continental alliances (teams). Using geometry tools and raw materials, players manufacture geometric products according to engineering specifications. These products are sold to banks to earn money, which is then taxed and used for national development or military expansion.

The objective is to maximize the combined economic and political power of the team before the game ends.

# Invasion

Military units may invade another team.

# Colonies

Successful invasions create colonies.

Colonies provide

- Extra taxes
- Extra resources
- Manufacturing bonuses

A colony belongs to the conquering team until recaptured or the game ends.

# Notes

This game is a fictional educational strategy simulation designed to teach geometry, manufacturing, economic systems, taxation, teamwork, and strategic planning. It is not intended to represent real-world politics or military conflict, and the invasion mechanics are abstract game elements similar to those found in many board and strategy games.
