# The Religion of Football
## *Futbolism (Religion of the Beautiful Game)*

> **Disclaimer:** This is a fictional world-building document created for entertainment and creative purposes. It is **not** intended to replace or mock any real-world religion, belief system, or philosophy.

---

# Motto

> **"Football Above All. Respect All. Play Fair. Unite the World."**

---

# Core Belief

Followers of Futbolism believe that football is humanity's greatest universal language.

Regardless of nationality, race, language, politics, or wealth, every person becomes equal once the match begins.

The football pitch is considered sacred because it teaches:

- Unity
- Teamwork
- Sacrifice
- Respect
- Passion
- Excellence
- Discipline
