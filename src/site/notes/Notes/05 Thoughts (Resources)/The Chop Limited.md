# The Chop Limited: A Global Bengali Food Empire

## Abstract

The Chop Limited is a fictional multinational food corporation focused on transforming the traditional Bengali snack "chop" into a globally recognized food category. With an estimated valuation of **$2.5 billion** and monthly revenue of approximately **$100 million**, the company operates through two major divisions:

1. **The Chop of India** — a national and international expansion brand valued at **$1 billion**.
2. **Bose-er Chop** — a premium Bengali heritage brand valued at **$1.5 billion**, focusing on authentic Bengal-style chops and cultural experiences.

The company combines food manufacturing, restaurant chains, licensing, entertainment partnerships, and cultural branding to create a global Bengali culinary ecosystem.

---

# 1. Introduction

Food companies have historically grown by transforming regional cuisines into global brands. Examples include pizza from Italy, sushi from Japan, and burgers from the United States.

The Chop Limited aims to achieve a similar transformation by taking the Bengali chop — a popular fried snack with varieties including vegetable chop, fish chop, chicken chop, egg chop, and innovative fusion versions — and establishing it as an internationally recognized food product.

The company's mission:

> "To make Bengali chop a global food identity."
