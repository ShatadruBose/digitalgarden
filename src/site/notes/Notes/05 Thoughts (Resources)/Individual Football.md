# ⚽ Individual Football

*A football format where every player is an independent competitor with their own career, identity, Elo, and salary.*

---

# Overview

Unlike traditional football, players do not permanently belong to clubs. Teams are created only for each match, while every player maintains their own long-term career.

Each player has:

- Unique jersey
- Unique jersey number
- Unique playstyle
- Unique position
- Unique strengths and weaknesses
- Overall Rating (OVR)
- Individual Elo Rating
- Salary paid by the Football Board
- Personal statistics and achievements

---

# Core Principles

- Every player is unique.
- No two players share the same jersey or number.
- Teams are temporary.
- Careers are permanent.
- Players compete for both team victories and personal success.

# Elo System

Every player owns an independent Elo Rating.

- Winning increases Elo.
- Losing decreases Elo.
- Defeating higher-rated players awards more Elo.
- Losing to lower-rated players removes more Elo.
- Elo belongs to the player, never the team.

---

# Salary System

Every player is employed by the **Football Board**, not by clubs.

Salary is calculated using:

- Elo Rating
- OVR
- Match Rating
- Goals
- Assists
- Saves
- Clean Sheets
- MVP Awards
- Fair Play
- Consistency

---

# Career Statistics

Each player records:

- Matches Played
- Wins
- Draws
- Losses
- Goals
- Assists
- Saves
- Tackles
- Interceptions
- Pass Accuracy
- MVP Awards
- Yellow Cards
- Red Cards
- Average Match Rating
- OVR
- Elo Rating
- Salary
- Total Career Earnings

---

# Match Formation

Every match consists of:

- 5 vs 5
- One Goalkeeper per team
- Four Outfield Players per team

Teams are temporary and may change completely for the next match.

---

# Match Objectives

Each player has two simultaneous goals:

1. Help the temporary team win.
2. Improve their personal career through better performances, higher Elo, and greater earnings.

---

# Philosophy

> "Ten players. Ten identities. Ten careers. Teams are temporary, but every player's legacy is permanent."
